> The EMR Offering MUST provide the ability to record and update all the CPP categories.

The guideline requires at least **Diagnosis, Procedures and Medication Summary** to be managed from the encounter and defines encounter as **SOAP Notes and Progress Notes**. Selecting/managing records from the note is a permitted approach; the three-category minimum is mandatory.
