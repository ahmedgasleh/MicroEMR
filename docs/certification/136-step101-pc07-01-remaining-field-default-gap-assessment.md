Important distinctions carried forward:

- Required supported elements do not mandate known values for every patient. A blank is not a negative finding, zero, Adult, cessation date or inferred diagnosis.
- An independently supported field can be retrieved in an appropriate full record; PC07.01 does not require every source field or every row on a compact card. A five-record card with count/navigation is not itself a defect.
- A declared field/property/RowVersion column is not evidence that writes validate it, nor that a transaction includes a required audit insert.
- **PC07.06 explicitly requires an ongoing medication treatment plan by default.** Acute medications may also be included. This does not prescribe a default classification for a new or legacy medication.
- Optional dates omitted from the approved Family/Risk minimum do not become new blockers. If optional dates are later supported, their specified partial/full date conformance still applies. Other categories' mandatory date support remains a real gap where only full dates exist.
- PC07.10 has a distinct minimum encounter-managed diagnosis/procedure/medication-summary obligation. A patient-chart editor, a Procedure encounter type or a finalized prescription does not automatically prove that entire obligation.
