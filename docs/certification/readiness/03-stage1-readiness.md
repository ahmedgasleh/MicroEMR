# Stage 1 and Vendor Readiness

## Submission matrix

| Artifact | Status | Owner/input needed |
|---|---|---|
| Cover Letter | NEEDS BUSINESS INPUT | Legal entity, product/release and authorized signatory. |
| Application for EMR Certification Form | NEEDS ONTARIOMD FORM | Current official form plus business/technical contacts. |
| Mandatory Document Checklist | NEEDS ONTARIOMD FORM | Current checklist and accountable owners. |
| EMR Requirements Attestation Form | NEEDS ONTARIOMD FORM | Exact release/requirements; executive and technical review. |
| Hosting Substantiation Requirements | NEEDS ONTARIOMD FORM | Hosting design, provider evidence and operations input. |
| Hosting Specification Reference Sheet | NEEDS ONTARIOMD FORM | Exact 1.3 template. |
| Product architecture description | PARTIAL | Repository architecture is documentable; production topology is not established. |
| Security architecture | PARTIAL | Product controls exist; operational controls/evidence remain. |
| Support/implementation/training model | NOT STARTED | Business and operations input. |
| Business continuity / disaster recovery | NEEDS TECHNICAL INPUT | Approved RPO/RTO, runbooks and test evidence. |
| Reference-site plan | NOT STARTED | Business, clinical and customer participation. |

## Business information required

Do not infer: legal vendor/entity name; product name/version; business, technical, support, privacy and security contacts; hosting provider/location; subprocessors; support hours/SLA; incident response; BCP/DR ownership; training and implementation approach; release/change management; reference-site candidates; commercial and certification-fee approvals.

## Readiness conclusion

Stage 1 paperwork is not submission-ready, but the technical product is mature enough to support an initial OntarioMD discussion and structured readiness program. Confirm current application availability before preparing official forms.
