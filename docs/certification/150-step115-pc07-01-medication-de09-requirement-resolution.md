## 7. Source, classification and status mapping

- **Ongoing / Acute / NULL:** Step 102 explicitly records treatment intent. NULL is Unclassified / unknown, including legacy; no backfill. Active is lifecycle, not intent, adherence, long-term therapy or reconciliation.
- **CPP default:** Active Ongoing only. Acute and unknown remain accessible in the full medication list and explicit selected-print scopes Ongoing/Acute/Unclassified/AllActive. No change to this accepted behavior is proposed.
- **Discontinued / inactive / entered in error:** retained list records accessible through lifecycle filtering; excluded from the Active summary. DE09.017 means discontinuation from treatment plan: Discontinued is a sound candidate. Confirm plan ownership/retrieval and unknown semantics; no duplicate flag is mandated. Acute is not past.
- **Finalized order:** evidence of a recorded prescription, not proof the patient takes it, complied, started treatment or discontinued it. Cancelled/Superseded are order lifecycle states, not medication-list cessation.
- **Patient-reported, imported, historical and non-prescription medications:** the existing list can record names/full dates without a local prescription, but no explicit source-kind/import-original-identity/reconciliation contract was established in these sources. DE09 defines no distinct provenance/import/reconciliation field or workflow. Broader source ownership/applicability still needs closure before claiming a complete medication list; no import/reconciliation system is justified here.
- No dispensing tracker, licensed catalogue, e-prescribing transport or reconciliation redesign is inferred from the existing refill elements or PC07.06 default.
